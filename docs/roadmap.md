# Roadmap

Status: **Approved by the owner (PR #1, 22/10/2026).**

This roadmap covers a secure, offline-first management application for one insurance
agency in Morocco working with **RMA Assurance** and **CAT Assurance & Réassurance**.
It must run on a single server on the office LAN, with no Internet needed for daily work.

## Ground rules

- One branch per step (`feature/…`, `fix/…`, `docs/…`) created from an up-to-date `main`.
  The owner commits, pushes and merges. The next step starts only after the previous one is merged.
- Nothing about an insurer (product, tariff, premium, form, eligibility, document list) is
  treated as fact unless it is recorded in `docs/insurer-rules.md` with a source, a date
  and a reviewer. Everything else is marked **requires confirmation**.
- The app never presents its own records as official insurer documents. Internal estimates,
  internal contracts and agency receipts are always labelled as internal.
- No real customer data in development, tests, screenshots or git. Demo data comes only from a seed script.
- Every step ships with tests and updated docs, and reports real check results.

### Phase 1 — Business and regulatory research (`docs/insurer-research`) — first pass done, waiting for agency documents
Results: `docs/insurer-rules.md` (register) and `docs/requirements.md` (requirements, agency and legal questions).

- Collect official public information from RMA, CAT, ACAPS and the Fédération Marocaine de l'Assurance (formerly FMSAR).
- Scope requested by the owner: **all lines**: car, motorcycle, taxi / passenger transport, commercial vehicles, fleets, transport.
- Build the verification register `docs/insurer-rules.md`: rule, insurer, source, date seen, status, still to confirm.
- List questions the agency or the insurers' representatives must answer (internal forms, document checklists, numbering, attestation handling).
- First list of legal questions for Law 09-08 / CNDP (to be answered by a qualified professional).

Known so far (preliminary web search, 22/10/2026):
- Official policy documents and insurance attestations are numbered and their formats are controlled
  (arrêté n° 213-05). The app must **record** their numbers and store scans, never generate them.
- ACAPS has enforced the correct attestation model per vehicle usage (e.g. electric vehicles = "Divers", white attestation).
  So the vehicle "usage" category matters and must come from verified rules.
- The paper constat amiable is still used; an e-constat exists, but only on agents constateurs' phones.
- RMA's public claims page lists steps (2526, constat, online space) but no document list or deadline.
- CAT's practical-information page has flyers only, and no subscription, renewal or claims document lists.

Done when: register exists with sources; unknowns listed; owner has the list of questions for the agency.

### Phase 8 — Documents, reports and printing (`feature/documents`, `feature/reports`)
**Owner's decision (24/10/2026):** the app follows the insurance; the information comes from the documents printed by the
RMA and CAT platforms (attestation, conditions particulières, quittance, note de couverture). It is captured by typing and
by scanning, typing first, so missing or wrong values can be corrected. Three steps:
1. `feature/documents`: scans attached to contracts, opened, replaced, removed with a reason. **Built.**
2. `feature/document-capture`: the printed fields typed beside the scan, per document type; the app compares them with
   the customer, vehicle, contract and attestation and lists what is missing or different. **Built:** the issued
   contract's policy number, dates and premium are corrected in one click (audited); other differences link to the
   customer or vehicle page.
3. `feature/attestation-scan` (owner, 24/10/2026: "scan the attestation, get its information and track this insurance";
   offline reading chosen): **built** for attestations. Conditions particulières, quittances and notes de couverture are
   still typed (step 2). Accuracy on filled documents to be measured with real filled scans.
- Secure upload (size, extension, file-signature checks), authorised download, replacement history.
- Optional local malware scan (ClamAV) if the server can maintain it.
- Local PDF generation (summaries, checklists, agency receipts) and printing.
- Reports and exports (`feature/reports`, **built**): the six reports of the agency's answers on screen for management
  and the auditor; CSV and Excel exports for management after re-authentication, audited. PDF printing of reports is
  not requested.
